# Background Patterns

**Monsoon Rain** is a pattern of short vertical lines, like falling rain, that runs down one edge
of a section. It's a quiet accent: it never covers the section, and text over it stays readable.

## Placement

The pattern sits in a band along the section's **start** edge or its **end** edge (left and right
in left-to-right languages). The band is 20% of the section's width, but never narrower than 75px
or wider than 200px, so it stays a small accent at every screen size. The pattern image is 300px
square and repeats down the band from the top.

## Color

The pattern takes its color from the surface it's on, through that surface's on-color group:

- **Default surface** (White): <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-default.pattern')} /> (Rain),
  with dark text.
- **Brand surface** (Blue): <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-brand.pattern')} /> (Rain),
  with White text.

These two combinations passed an accessibility review: White text on Blue keeps about 6.0:1 contrast
where the pattern is darkest behind it, and dark text on White about 16.8:1. Test any other
section color before using the pattern on it.

## The asset

`monsoon-rain.svg` ships with the tokens package at `assets/patterns/monsoon-rain.svg`. It's a mask,
not a picture: it holds the pattern's shape and its 40% opacity, and the surface's pattern color
paints it. One file works on every surface, and a theme can change the color without a new file.
