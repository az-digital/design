# Background patterns

Monsoon Rain places a repeating image along one edge of a section. It was originally called Monsoon Sky.

<Canvas of={PatternStories.Examples} sourceState="hidden" />

<Controls of={PatternStories.Examples} />

These examples use Arizona Bootstrap v5.3.0's background wrapper on colors offered in its
[background wrappers demo](https://review.digital.arizona.edu/arizona-bootstrap/main/docs/5.2/components/background-wrappers/).
They do not establish which surfaces are approved. Check text contrast for each use.
The release does **not** apply the text-legibility backdrop to Monsoon Rain: its mixin
is used on Trilines only. The backdrop below records the requested design decision, not
behavior demonstrated by this release.

## Image and placement

Use the same <a href={monsoonSky} download>Monsoon Sky SVG</a> for either
the start or end edge. The original image is also available to implementations at
`@az-digital/tokens/assets/patterns/monsoon-sky.svg`.
The band repeats vertically at 300px and is 20% of the section width, clamped to
75–200px. The image is 40% opaque; section content is not faded.

## Text legibility

Paragraphs, list items and rules on a patterned section have a backdrop of the section's
color at 70% opacity; use White when no section color is set.

<TokenDisplay items={[getTokenData('az.opacity.pattern-text-backdrop')]} layout="table" />
