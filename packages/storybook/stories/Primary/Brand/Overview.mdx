# Brand overview

The University of Arizona brand is the shared identity that makes every university experience feel unmistakably Arizona. This section explains how that brand carries into digital products, so designers and developers can build interfaces that look, sound, and behave like the university.

The brand itself is owned and maintained by University Marketing & Communications. The official [brand guidelines](https://marcom.arizona.edu/brand-guidelines) remain the source of truth for brand strategy, logos, photography, and print and marketing materials. This design system does not replace the official brand guidelines; it translates the guidelines into tokens, components, and patterns for digital products.

## Brand pillars

The university's brand rests on four pillars. Use them to guide your design decisions:

- **An enduring legacy** — Founded in 1885, the university is the most storied institution in the Southwest.
- **Shaped by the Sonoran Desert** — The desert landscape informs our palette, textures, and sense of place.
- **Excellence without pretense** — World-class work, presented in a way that stays approachable and clear.
- **Bear Down, build up** — Resolve and tenacity, working together to move past challenges.

## Personality

The brand is confident, optimistic, compassionate, curious, and determined. In digital products, that means interfaces should be clear and direct, welcoming to every audience, and accessible by default.

## How the brand shows up in the design system

- **Color** — The brand palette, including Arizona Red and Arizona Blue, is defined as design tokens. Explore the <a href="./?path=/docs/primary-brand-color--docs" target="_top">Brand: Color</a> page and see <a href="./?path=/docs/primary-tokens-overview--docs" target="_top">Tokens</a> for the full token system.
- **Typography** — Brand typefaces apply to body text, headings, and components. Components, such as buttons and cards, come with defined type styles. See the official [typography guidelines](https://marcom.arizona.edu/brand-guidelines/typography).
- **Voice and content** — Writing guidance lives in [Content Design](?path=/docs/primary-foundations-content-design--docs).
- **Components** — Every component is built on these foundations, so using the components keeps a product on brand.
