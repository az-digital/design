# Button

An action in a form, a dialog or a page: a label, and an optional icon, in a fully rounded
shape.

## Colors

Each color is for a background. The button's contract (`button.contract.json`) points
every color straight at a brand color.

- **On White, Cool Gray or Warm Gray:**
  - **Red,** the primary button: <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.red')} /> with
    White text, turning <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.sonoran-red')} /> on
    hover.
  - **Outline Red:** a Red border and Red text, filling Red with White text on hover.
- **On Blue or Azurite:**
  - **Rain:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.rain')} /> with Blue text, turning
    a darker Rain (`#65c0db`) with <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.tinta')} />
    text on hover.
  - **Outline Rain:** a Rain border and Rain text, filling Rain with Tinta text on hover.
- **On Red:**
  - **White, Red text:** White with Red text, turning
    <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.cloud')} /> on hover.
  - **Outline White, Red text:** a White border and White text, filling White with Red
    text on hover.
- **On Oasis:**
  - **White, Blue text:** White with Blue text, turning Cloud on hover.
  - **Outline White, Blue text:** a White border and White text, filling White with Blue
    text on hover.

- **Keyboard focus:** a focused button looks the way it does on hover, with a ring 2px
  outside it. The ring is <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.bougainvillea')} /> on
  the Red buttons and White on every other color, and its width is the shared
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width')} />.
- **Rain's hover fill** (`#65c0db`) isn't in the brand palette yet. It's recorded in the
  contract as a value, with a note saying so.

## Size

- **Default:** 46px tall: 18px text on a 24px line, with 9px of padding above and below
  and 28px to each side.
- **Small:** 29px tall: 16px text on a 19px line, with 3px above and below and 11px to
  each side.
- **Large:** 55px tall: 22px text on a 27px line, with 12px above and below and 34px to
  each side, and 0.18px letter spacing instead of 0.14px.

Every size has a 2px border, so outline and solid buttons line up, and fully rounded ends.
The label is Proxima Nova Condensed Bold. An icon sits 4px from the label and doesn't
change the button's height. Colors change over 150ms, ease-in-out, which hasn't been
reviewed by design yet and turns off when someone asks for reduced motion.
