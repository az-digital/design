# Button

An action in a form, a dialog or a page: a label, and an optional icon, in a fully rounded
shape.

## Colors

The button's contract (`button.contract.json`) has a solid and an outline button for each
brand color, each for a background, and for each of Bootstrap's role colors.

- **On White, Cool Gray or Warm Gray:**
  - **Red,** the primary button: <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.red')} /> with
    White text, turning <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.sonoran-red')} /> on
    hover. **Outline Red** fills Red on hover.
  - **Blue:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.blue')} /> with White text,
    turning <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.azurite')} /> on hover. **Outline
    Blue** fills Blue on hover. Blue isn't in the designs yet.
- **On Blue or Azurite:** **Rain,** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.rain')} />
  with Blue text, turning a darker Rain (`#65c0db`) with
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.tinta')} /> text on hover. **Outline Rain**
  fills Rain with Tinta text on hover.
- **On Red:** **White, Red text,** turning
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.cloud')} /> on hover, and **outline White,
  Red text,** which fills White with Red text on hover.
- **On Oasis:** **White, Blue text,** turning Cloud on hover, and **outline White, Blue
  text,** which fills White with Blue text on hover.
- **Role colors:** success, danger, warning, info, light and dark, each solid and
  outline. Their colors are Bootstrap's, as Arizona Bootstrap ships them, until marketing
  approves status colors; only info (Rain) and the text on some of them are brand colors.

- **Keyboard focus:** a focused button looks the way it does on hover, with a ring 2px
  outside it. Its width is the shared <Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width')} />.
  The ring is <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.bougainvillea')} /> on the Red
  buttons, White on outline Rain and the White buttons, and the button's own color on the
  others.
- **Colors that aren't brand colors,** such as Rain's hover fill and the role colors, are
  recorded in the contract as values, each with a note saying why.
- **Class names:** each color's description in the contract gives its Arizona Bootstrap
  classes: today's color-named and role classes, and the semantic names proposed in
  az-digital/arizona-bootstrap#2381 (`.btn-primary-2`, `.btn-secondary-4`,
  `.btn-inverse-primary`…).

## Size

- **Default:** 46px tall: 18px text on a 24px line, with 9px of padding above and below
  and 28px to each side.
- **Small:** 29px tall: 16px text on a 19px line, with 3px above and below and 11px to
  each side.
- **Large:** 55px tall: 22px text on a 27px line, with 12px above and below and 34px to
  each side, and 0.18px letter spacing instead of 0.14px.

Every size has a 2px border, so outline and solid buttons line up, and fully rounded ends.
The label is Proxima Nova Condensed Bold. An icon sits 4px from the label and doesn't
change the button's height. Colors change over 150ms, ease-in-out, which hasn't been
reviewed by design yet and turns off when someone asks for reduced motion.
