# Accordions

A stack of titles that each open to show more content. Each title has a circled plus
icon on the right, which becomes a minus while the item is open.

## Colors come from the surface

An accordion sits on the default surface, <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.surface.default')} />
(White), with no fill of its own, and takes its colors from that surface's on-color group:

- **Titles and their icons:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-default.link-supporting')} />
  (Azurite), and <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-default.link-supporting-hover')} />
  (Blue) on hover and while the item is open.
- **The line under each title:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-default.border')} />,
  a neutral gray at 50% opacity.
- **Keyboard focus:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-default.focus-ring')} />
  (Bougainvillea), a 2px ring 2px outside the title.
- **The Copy link:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-default.link')} /> (Red),
  and <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-default.link-hover')} /> (Sonoran Red)
  on hover, like other links.

## Shape and spacing

- **Titles:** no space to the left or right, so they line up with the text around
  them, and 16px above and below. A 1px line runs under each one.
- **Body:** no space to the left or right, and 30px above and below (40px from 768px up).

## Sizes

Every size is Semibold, except Small, which is Bold. Large is the default.

Each size sets the title's text size and line height, its letter spacing, the gap
between the title and its icon, the icon's diameter and the length of its lines.

- **Large, below 768px:** 34px text on a 38px line, 0.09px letter spacing, a 24px gap,
  a 44px icon with 25px lines.
- **Large, from 768px up:** 44px text on a 52px line, 0.12px letter spacing, a 27px gap,
  a 50px icon with 29px lines.
- **Medium:** 28px text on a 36px line, no letter spacing, a 22px gap, a 40px icon with
  23px lines.
- **Small:** 22px text on a 28.8px line, no letter spacing, a 16px gap, a 30px icon with
  18px lines.

## Typefaces

Titles are serif by default (<Token data={getTokenData('az.font-family.serif')} />),
or sans serif (<Token data={getTokenData('az.font-family.sans')} />) for a lighter look,
such as an accordion in a narrow text column.

## The icon

A circle with a plus inside, drawn in the title's color. Its circle and lines are 2px
thick, and 3px on hover and while the item is open, when the plus becomes a minus.
The lines have rounded ends.

## Copy link

An accordion item can end with a **Copy link**: a link icon followed by the label
"Copy link", 8px below the body's content.

- **Look:** a 24px icon, 6px before the label. Only the label is underlined, 4.5px below
  the text (0.25em at the 18px body size).
- **Copying:** clicking it copies the item's URL. The icon changes to a check, and a
  "Copied!" message appears; it shows on click only, never on hover. The label doesn't
  change. Screen readers announce that the link was copied, and it works without a mouse.
- **Opening a linked item:** visiting that URL opens the item and scrolls its title to
  the top of the screen, smoothly. It stops at the page's scroll offset,
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.dimension.semantic.page.scroll-offset')} /> (0px), which a
  fixed header raises so it doesn't cover the title.
- Anchored accordions work best when opening one item doesn't close the others.
