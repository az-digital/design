# Card

Card is a flexible content container — a filled box with optional header, image, icon,
title/subtitle/text/links, and footer. Purely presentational: no real interactive
behavior, so unlike Accordion/Tabs, this component is hand-rolled rather than wrapping
`react-bootstrap`.

## Content types

- **Titles, text, and links** — `title`/`subtitle`/`text`/`links`. Links stack at the
  end of the body, in a `.card-links` group.
- **Image cap** (`image`) — `.card-img-top`, matching Arizona Bootstrap's own naming
  (top only; there's no `.card-img-bottom` prop here — not demonstrated as a distinct
  need yet).
- **Icon** (`icon`) — `.card-icon`, at the top of the body above the title.
- **Header and footer** — `header`/`footer`.

## Card styles in Figma

The Figma Cards page defines three styles, each built from the props above:

- **Non-Media** — `title` and `text`.
- **Icon** — `icon`, `title`, and `text`. Its title takes its own color.
- **Media** — `image`, `title`, and `text`, with or without `links`.

Each style comes in two widths, 3 and 4 across a 1296px container. The width comes
from the grid column a card sits in, not from the card, so the stories show one
width each.

## Not covered by this component (yet)

Arizona Bootstrap's cards docs page covers considerably more ground than this first
pass. Deliberately deferred, not silently dropped:

- **List groups inside a card** (`.list-group.list-group-flush`) and the **"Kitchen
  sink"** example combining image + body + list group + links.
- **Navigation inside a card header** (`.nav-tabs`/`.nav-pills` in `.card-header`) —
  ties into [`Nav`](?path=/docs/primary-components-navigation-nav--docs)/[`Tabs`](?path=/docs/primary-components-containers-tabs--docs);
  `custom/_card.scss` has real styling fixes specifically for this combination.
- **Image overlays**, **horizontal cards**.
- **Layout patterns**: card groups, CSS-grid cards, masonry.
- **Sizing** via grid markup, utilities, or custom CSS; **text alignment**;
  **background and color** variants.
- **Clickable Cards** and **Title on Image** — both confirmed
  **Arizona-custom** in the docs ("Custom Arizona Bootstrap Styling"), plus the
  deprecated `.card-clickable`/`.card-landing-grid` classes in
  `scss/deprecated/_card.scss`.

## Tokens

`az.component.card.*` takes its values from the Figma Cards page (Non-Media, Media,
and Icon cards): a borderless card on a <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.surface.subtle')} />
surface, with 24px body padding, 16px corners, and one 12px gap between title, text,
and links. Values shared by every style sit at the root. The `icon` group holds only
what the Icon card changes: its title color and the space between icon and title.

The header and footer padding (`cap.*`) still comes from Arizona Bootstrap, because
Figma doesn't design a card header or footer yet.
