# Card

A container for one piece of content: a title and text, with an icon, a photo, a list of
links or a call to action.

## Kinds of cards

- **Clickable cards:** the whole card is one link, and its colors change on hover.
  - **Text only:** a title and text.
  - **Icon:** an icon above the title.
  - **Image and text:** a photo above the title, which zooms in on hover.
- **Title on image:** a clickable square photo with only a title on it. The photo zooms
  in on hover.
- **Link list:** a title, text and a list of links. The card isn't a link itself, so it
  has no hover colors.
- **Call to action:** a title, text and a button or a row of social links. The card isn't
  a link itself, so it has no hover colors.

Cards may also have a header above the body and a footer below it.

## Design decisions

Three contracts hold the card's design decisions. The card's contract
(`card.contract.json`) sets what every card shares. The clickable card's contract
(`clickable-card.contract.json`) adds the hover colors, and the title on image card's
contract (`title-on-image-card.contract.json`) adds its square photo and title. The
card's fill comes from the card background tokens (`az.color.bg.card.*`), its other
colors are brand colors, and its sizes and timings are the cards' own values, written
in the contracts.

### Every card

- **Fill:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.bg.card.cloud')} />, with no border: the
  fill sets the card apart from the page. In a forced colors (high contrast) mode, which
  removes fills, the card draws a 1px border instead.
- **Shape:** 16px corners.
- **Padding:** 24px between the card's edges and its content. The header and footer have
  24px to the left and right, so their text lines up with the body's, and 12px above and
  below.
- **Title:** 22px Bold, on a 30px line, in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.azurite')} />. 12px below the title's line,
  before the text.
- **Text:** 18px Regular, on a 28px line, in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.tinta')} />: the same size as body text.
  12px below the text, before more text or a list of links.

### Clickable cards

- **The whole card is one link:** the title is a link that covers the card.
- **Hover:** the fill turns <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.bg.card.cloud-hover')} />, the text
  and other links turn <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.white')} />, and the
  title and icon turn <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.rain')} />. The title is
  underlined.
- **Keyboard focus:** when <kbd>Tab</kbd> reaches the title link or another link in the
  card, the card shows its hover colors. The focused link's ring is drawn in its own color
  (Rain on the title, White on other links), so the ring stands out from the fill as much
  as the link's text does. Its width is the
  shared <Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width')} /> (2px).
- **Touch screens:** on a device that can't hover, such as a phone, the title is always
  underlined.
- **Motion:** the colors fade over 300ms, ease-out. The fade turns off when someone asks
  for reduced motion; the colors still change.
- **Icon:** 64px, in <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.azurite')} />, with 16px
  between it and the title's line. Use an icon font glyph at that font size, or an SVG at
  that width and height with `fill="currentColor"`. The icon is decorative, so hide it
  from screen readers with `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Photo:** 5:3, cropped to fill that shape, in the card's top corners. On hover and with
  keyboard focus, it zooms in 8% over 250ms, ease, and zooms back out the same way. It
  stays inside the card's corners. The zoom still happens when someone asks for reduced
  motion, without the animation.

### Title on image

- **Photo:** square, cropped to fill it. It zooms the same way as a clickable card's photo.
- **Title:** the card's title type, in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.white')} />, at the bottom of the photo, 24px
  from its left, right and bottom edges. Behind it, a gradient covers the bottom half of
  the photo, from transparent to <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.blue')} /> at
  the bottom, so the title stays readable on any photo.
- **Hover and keyboard focus:** the title is underlined. Its colors don't change: the
  photo covers the card's fill. Its focus ring is White.

### Link list

- **Links:** 18px Semibold, on a 28px line, in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.azurite')} />, underlined, in Proxima Nova
  Condensed. One per line, 12px apart.

### Call to action

- **Space:** 32px between the text and the call to action.
- **Button:** a default size Red button.
- **Social links:** 45px icons in <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.red')} />,
  8px apart. Each link names its site for screen readers.
