# Icon Card

A Link Card with an icon above the title, which says what the page is about. The whole
card is the link.

<Canvas of={IconCardStories.Default} sourceState="none" />

<Controls of={IconCardStories.Default} />

The card above is Arizona Bootstrap's 5.3.x branch. Hover it, or press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to
reach its title link, for its hover colors. The icons are the outline style of each icon,
at a light weight, to match arizona.edu.

## When to use it

- **Use it** in a row of cards that each go to a part of the site, such as Future
  Students, Current Students and Alumni, where an icon helps people find theirs.
- **Pick an icon for the page, not for decoration.** Screen readers skip it, so the title
  has to say everything on its own.
- **Use icon cards together:** in a row, give every card an icon, or none.
- **Don't put a button or other links in it:** the whole card is already one link.

## Design decisions

- **Icon:** 60px, in <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.azurite')} />, with 16px
  between it and the title's line. Use an icon font glyph at that font size, or an SVG at
  that width and height with `fill="currentColor"`. The icon is decorative, so hide it
  from screen readers with `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Fill:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.bg.card.cloud')} />, with no border. In a
  forced colors (high contrast) mode, which removes fills, the card draws a 1px border
  instead.
- **Title:** 22px Bold, on a 30px line, in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.azurite')} />. 8px below the title's line.
- **Text:** 18px Regular, on a 28px line, in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.tinta')} />.
- **Shape and padding:** 16px corners, and 24px between the card's edges and its content.
- **Hover:** the fill turns <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.bg.card.cloud-hover')} />,
  the text turns <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.white')} />, and the icon and
  title turn <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.rain')} />. The title is
  underlined.
- **Keyboard focus:** when <kbd>Tab</kbd> reaches the title link, the card shows its hover
  colors, and the link's ring is Rain. Its width is the shared
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width.md')} /> (2px).
- **Touch screens:** on a device that can't hover, such as a phone, the title is always
  underlined.
- **Motion:** the colors fade over 300ms, ease-out. The fade turns off when someone asks
  for reduced motion; the colors still change.
