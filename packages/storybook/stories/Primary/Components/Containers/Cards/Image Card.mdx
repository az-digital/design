# Image Card

A Link Card with a photo above the title. The whole card is the link, and the photo
zooms in on hover.

<Canvas of={ImageCardStories.Default} sourceState="none" />

<Controls of={ImageCardStories.Default} />

The card above is Arizona Bootstrap's 5.3.x branch. Hover it, or press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to
reach its title link, for its hover colors and zoom.

## When to use it

- **Use it** when a photo shows what the page is about, such as a program or a place.
- **Crop the photo to 5:3,** and keep what matters away from the edges: it zooms in on
  hover.
- **The photo is decorative** when the title and text say what it shows, so leave its
  `alt` empty. Describe it only when it adds something the text doesn't.
- **Don't put a button or other links in it:** the whole card is already one link.

## Design decisions

- **Photo:** 5:3, cropped to fill that shape, in the card's top corners. On hover and with
  keyboard focus, it zooms in 8% over 250ms, ease, and zooms back out the same way. It
  stays inside the card's corners. The zoom still happens when someone asks for reduced
  motion, without the animation.
- **Fill:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.bg.card.cloud')} />, with no border. In a
  forced colors (high contrast) mode, which removes fills, the card draws a 1px border
  instead.
- **Title:** 22px Bold, on a 30px line, in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.azurite')} />. 8px below the title's line.
- **Text:** 18px Regular, on a 28px line, in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.tinta')} />.
- **Shape and padding:** 16px corners, and 24px between the photo or the card's edges and
  its content.
- **Hover:** the fill turns <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.bg.card.cloud-hover')} />,
  the text turns <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.white')} />, and the title turns
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.rain')} /> and is underlined.
- **Keyboard focus:** when <kbd>Tab</kbd> reaches the title link, the card shows its hover
  colors and zoom, and the link's ring is Rain. Its width is the shared
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width.md')} /> (2px).
- **Touch screens:** on a device that can't hover, such as a phone, the title is always
  underlined.
- **Motion:** the colors fade over 300ms, ease-out. The fade turns off when someone asks
  for reduced motion; the colors still change.
