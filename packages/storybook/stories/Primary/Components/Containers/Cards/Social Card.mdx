# Social Card

A title, text and a row of social media links. The card isn't a link itself: each icon
is.

<Canvas of={SocialCardStories.Default} sourceState="none" />

<Controls of={SocialCardStories.Default} />

The card above is Arizona Bootstrap's 5.3.x branch. Pick the sites under Sites.

Arizona Bootstrap has no styles for a card that isn't a link yet, so until its updates land the card above doesn't match every value on this page: its title is Blue, not Azurite, and its text is gray, not Tinta. Its icons are 18px, not 45px, and 16px below the text, not 32px.

## When to use it

- **Use it** to point people to the social media channels for a unit or the university.
- **Link only to channels that are kept up.**
- **Each link names its site** for screen readers, such as "Instagram", because the icon
  has no text.

## Design decisions

- **Social links:** 45px icons in <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.red')} />,
  8px apart, 32px below the text.
- **Fill:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.bg.card.cloud')} />, with no border. In a
  forced colors (high contrast) mode, which removes fills, the card draws a 1px border
  instead.
- **Title:** 22px Bold, on a 30px line, in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.azurite')} />. 8px below the title's line.
- **Text:** 18px Regular, on a 28px line, in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.tinta')} />.
- **Shape and padding:** 16px corners, and 24px between the card's edges and its content.
- **No hover colors:** the card isn't a link, so only each icon reacts to hover and
  focus.
