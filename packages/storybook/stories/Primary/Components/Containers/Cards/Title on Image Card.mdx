# Title on Image Card

A square photo with only a title on it. The whole card is the link, and the photo zooms in
on hover.

<Canvas of={TitleOnImageCardStories.Default} sourceState="none" />

<Controls of={TitleOnImageCardStories.Default} />

The card above is Arizona Bootstrap's 5.3.x branch, with the markup in its Title on Image
docs. Hover it, or press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to reach its title link, for its zoom and
underline.

## When to use it

- **Use it** in a grid of choices that a photo and a word or two can explain, such as
  Undergraduate, Graduate and Online.
- **Keep the title short:** one to three words. There's no text, so the title has to say
  where the card goes.
- **Use any photo:** it's cropped to a square, and the gradient keeps the title readable.
  Keep what matters in the top half, above the title.
- **The photo is decorative,** so leave its `alt` empty: the title says where the card
  goes.

## Design decisions

- **Photo:** square, cropped to fill it. On hover and with keyboard focus, it zooms in 8%
  over 250ms, ease, and zooms back out the same way, inside the card's 16px corners. The
  zoom still happens when someone asks for reduced motion, without the animation.
- **Title:** 22px Bold, on a 30px line, in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.white')} />, at the bottom of the photo, 24px
  from its left, right and bottom edges. Behind it, a gradient covers the bottom half of
  the photo, from transparent to <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.blue')} /> at
  the bottom, so the title stays readable on any photo.
- **Hover and keyboard focus:** the photo zooms and the title is underlined. Its colors
  don't change: the photo covers the card. The title link's focus ring is White, so it
  shows on the gradient. Its width is the shared
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width.md')} /> (2px).
- **No fill or border:** the photo covers the whole card.
