# Checkbox

A box to turn one option on or off, beside its label.

<Canvas of={CheckboxStories.Default} sourceState="none" />

<Controls of={CheckboxStories.Default} />

The checkbox above is the current release. It matches this page. Valid and invalid states aren't recorded yet: their colors need marketing's approval.

## Colors

- **Unchecked:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.white')} />, with a <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.gray.600')} /> border.
- **Checked:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.red')} />, with a <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.white')} /> check.
- **Indeterminate** (some of a group's options chosen): <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.red')} />, with a <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.white')} /> dash.
- **Keyboard focus:** the box's border turns <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.bougainvillea')} />, with a ring around it in the same color at 70% opacity, <Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width.lg')} /> (4px) wide.
- **Pressed:** the box darkens to 90% brightness.
- **Disabled:** the box and its label are at 50% opacity.

## Size and spacing

- **Box:** 18px square with 4.5px corners, with a <Token data={getTokenData('az.border.width.sm')} /> (1px) border, 5px down from the top so it lines up with the first line of the label.
- **Label:** 18px on a 28px line, <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.gray.900')} />.
- **Spacing:** each option is at least 28px tall, with <Token data={getTokenData('az.spacing.025')} /> (2px) below it.
- **Label offset:** the label starts 27px from the box's left edge: the 18px box and 9px after it.
