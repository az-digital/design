# Input Group

A text field with something attached: a text add-on such as @ or a currency sign, a button
such as Subscribe, or both, so they read as one control.

<Canvas of={InputGroupStories.Default} sourceState="none" />

<Controls of={InputGroupStories.Default} />

The input group above is the current release. It matches this page.

## Colors

The input group's contract (`input-group.contract.json`) points each color at a brand
color:

- **Text:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.gray.900')} />, and the placeholder
  is the same color at 75% opacity.
- **Field:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.white')} />, with a
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.gray.600')} /> border.
- **Add-on:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.gray.100')} />, with the same
  border.
- **Button:** any of the Button's colors. The input group only sets its size and shape.
- **Keyboard focus:** the field's border turns
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.bougainvillea')} />, with a ring around it in
  the same color at 70% opacity, <Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width.lg')} />
  (4px) wide. It fades in over 150ms, ease-in-out, which hasn't been reviewed by design
  yet and turns off when someone asks for reduced motion.
- **Disabled:** the field fills <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.gray.200')} />,
  with a <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.gray.400')} /> border.

## Size

The field, add-ons and button share one height and border. Where two touch, their
corners are square.

- **Padding:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.spacing.075')} /> (6px) above and below the
  text, and <Token data={getTokenData('az.spacing.150')} /> (12px) to each side, in the
  field, add-ons and button alike.
- **Type:** 18px <Token data={getTokenData('az.font-weight.normal')} /> (Regular) on a 28px
  line, in the field and add-ons. The button keeps its own type.
- **Border:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.border.width.sm')} /> (1px), with
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.radius.2')} /> (6px) corners on the outside ends.

The input group has no design tokens of its own: values without one are in its contract.

## Search

The search form in a site's navigation is built like an input group, but its look is its
own, so it has its own contract (`search.contract.json`).

<Canvas of={InputGroupStories.Search} sourceState="none" />

The search form above is the current release. It matches this section.

- **Colors:** text, placeholder, border, divider and icon are
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.white')} />, on the Blue it's designed for. The
  icon turns <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.rain')} /> on hover, and the border and
  divider turn Rain while the field or button has focus, fading over 150ms, ease-in-out.
- **Height and corners:** 35px tall with <Token data={getTokenData('az.radius.1')} /> (4px)
  corners below 992px, and 43px with <Token data={getTokenData('az.radius.3')} /> (8px)
  from 992px up.
- **Border:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.border.width.md')} /> (2px), around the field
  and button together, with a divider of the same width between them.
- **Field:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.spacing.075')} /> (6px) of padding above and
  below its text and <Token data={getTokenData('az.spacing.150')} /> (12px) to each side;
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.font-size.root')} /> (16px)
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.font-weight.normal')} /> (Regular) type on a 22px line.
- **Button:** an icon only, 24px, with 9px of padding around it.
- **Form:** up to 265px wide, with 5px above and below it and 15px to each side.
- **Small screens:** the button that shows the search form has a keyboard focus ring in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.rain')} />,
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width.sm')} /> (1px) wide.
