# Input Group

A text field with an attached button, such as a search form, a newsletter signup or an
email field. The search form in a site's navigation is the first one designed.

<Canvas of={InputGroupStories.Default} sourceState="none" />

<Controls of={InputGroupStories.Default} />

The search form above is the current release, on the Blue it's designed for. It matches
this page.

## Colors

These colors come from the search form on a Blue navbar, the only design so far. The
input group's contract (`input-group.contract.json`) points each one at a color design
token:

- **Text, placeholder and icon:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.white')} />
- **Border, and the divider between field and button:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.white')} />
- **Hover and focus:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.rain')} />. The icon turns
  Rain on hover; the border and divider turn Rain while the field or button has focus,
  fading over 150ms, ease-in-out. That timing hasn't been reviewed by design yet, and it
  turns off when someone asks for reduced motion.

When a design puts the input group on another background, such as a White page, a
variant for it holds only the colors that change.

## Size

- **Height:** 35px below 992px, and 43px from 992px up.
- **Corners:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.radius.1')} /> (4px) below 992px, and
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.radius.3')} /> (8px) from 992px up.
- **Border:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.border.width.md')} /> (2px), around the field
  and button together, with a divider of the same width between them.
- **Field:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.spacing.075')} /> (6px) of padding above and
  below its text, and <Token data={getTokenData('az.spacing.150')} /> (12px) to the left and
  right.
- **Type:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.font-size.root')} /> (16px)
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.font-weight.normal')} /> (Regular), with a 22px line
  height.
- **Button:** a 24px icon with 9px of padding around it.

The input group has no design tokens of its own: values without one are in its contract.

## Search

The search form is an input group, so it adds only what's specific to search: it's up
to 265px wide, with 5px above and below it and 15px to each side. On small screens, the
button that shows it has a keyboard focus ring in
<Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.rain')} />,
<Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width.sm')} /> (1px) wide. These values are in the
search contract (`search.contract.json`).
