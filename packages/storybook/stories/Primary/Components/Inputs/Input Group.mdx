# Input Group

A text field with an attached button, such as a search form, a newsletter signup or an
email field. The search form in a site's navigation is the first one designed.

## Colors

These colors come from the search form on a Blue navbar, the only design so far. Each
points straight at a brand color:

- **Text and icon:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.component.input-group.field.color')} /> (White)
- **Border, and the divider between field and button:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.component.input-group.border.color')} /> (White)
- **Placeholder:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.component.input-group.field.placeholder.color')} /> (White)
- **Hover and focus:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.component.input-group.focus.border.color')} /> (Rain). The
  icon turns Rain on hover; the border and divider turn Rain while the field or button
  has focus, fading over 150ms.

When a design puts the input group on another background, such as a White page, a
variant for it holds only the colors that change.

## Shape is the same everywhere

- **Size:** 43px tall. The field has 6px of padding above and below its text, and 12px
  to the left and right.
- **Border:** 2px, around the field and button together, with 8px corners and a 2px
  divider between them.
- **Type:** 16px Regular, with a 22px line height.
- **Button:** a 24px icon with 9px of padding around it.

## Search

The search form is an input group, so it adds only what's specific to search: it's up
to 265px wide, with 5px above and below it and 15px to each side. On small screens, the
button that shows it has a 1px keyboard focus ring in the accent color.
