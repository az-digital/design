# Radio

A circle to pick one option from a group, beside its label. Choosing one clears the others in its group.

<Canvas of={RadioStories.Default} sourceState="none" />

<Controls of={RadioStories.Default} />

The group of radios above is the current release. It matches this page. Valid and invalid states aren't recorded yet: their colors need marketing's approval.

## Colors

- **Not chosen:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.white')} />, with a <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.gray.600')} /> border.
- **Chosen:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.red')} />, with a <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.white')} /> dot half the circle's size (9px).
- **Keyboard focus:** the circle's border turns <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.bougainvillea')} />, with a ring around it in the same color at 70% opacity, <Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width.lg')} /> (4px) wide.
- **Pressed:** the circle darkens to 90% brightness.
- **Disabled:** the circle and its label are at 50% opacity.

## Size and spacing

- **Circle:** 18px, fully round (<Token data={getTokenData('az.radius.pill')} />), with a <Token data={getTokenData('az.border.width.sm')} /> (1px) border, 5px down from the top so it lines up with the first line of the label.
- **Label:** 18px on a 28px line, <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.gray.900')} />.
- **Spacing:** each option is at least 28px tall, with <Token data={getTokenData('az.spacing.025')} /> (2px) below it.
- **Label offset:** the label starts 27px from the circle's left edge: the 18px circle and 9px after it.
