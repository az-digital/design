# Arizona Header

## Colors

- **Blue** (default): the current, recommended header color.
- **Red**: the original header color. It's being retired, so don't use it for new sites.

## Design decisions

The header's contract (`brand-header.contract.json`) sets each of its parts' design
properties: its colors are brand colors, its focus ring width is the shared one, and its
sizes and timings are the header's own values, written in the contract. The header has
no tokens of its own.

- **Fill:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.blue')} />. The original red header
  isn't a separate set of tokens: its contract points the fill at Red, and keeps every
  other color White. The red header is only needed until it's retired.
- **Size:** the header is at least 50px tall, and exactly 50px below 992px. The wordmark
  is 211 × 16px below 576px and 266.41 × 19.8px from 576px up, 10px from the header's
  left edge.
- **Keyboard focus:** the logo and header buttons draw a ring inside their edges, with
  2px corners, in <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.white')} />. Its width is the
  shared focus ring width, <Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width')} /> (2px).
- **Header buttons** (for adding search and menu toggles on mobile): at least 60px
  wide and as tall as the header, with 4px of padding above and below.
  - **Label:** text in <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.white')} />, 10px with a
    10px line height, 2px below the icon.
  - **Icon:** 24px square, the same color as the label. Use an SVG (inline or from a
    sprite) with `fill="currentColor"` so it picks up that color.
- **Logo:** the wordmark is <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.white')} />. It's a
  single color, so its color can change with the fill behind it. The contract wires it
  separately from the labels, so changing the text color doesn't recolor the logo.
- **Motion:** the off-canvas panel the header buttons open slides in over 300ms,
  ease-in-out, and the backdrop behind it fades in over 150ms. Header buttons change
  color and show their focus ring over 150ms. They haven't been
  reviewed by design yet, and all of them turn off when someone asks for reduced
  motion.
- **Fixed on mobile:** when the header is pinned to the top of the screen below 992px,
  linked sections stop 58px below the top when scrolled to, so the header doesn't
  cover them.
