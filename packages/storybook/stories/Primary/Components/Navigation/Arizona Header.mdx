# Arizona Header

The University of Arizona wordmark header, required on all `arizona.edu` subdomains.
It's a custom Arizona Bootstrap component, with no equivalent in upstream Bootstrap.

## Colors

- **Blue** (default): the current, recommended header. Arizona Bootstrap made it the
  default, and took it out of experimental status, in 5.2
  (az-digital/arizona-bootstrap#2299).
- **Red**: the original header color. It's still available, but Arizona Bootstrap plans
  to deprecate it, so don't use it for new sites.

## Tokens

`az.component.arizona-header.*` records the header's design decisions, every value
taken from Arizona Bootstrap:

- **Fill:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.component.arizona-header.container.color')} />,
  through the <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.surface.primary-2')} /> surface
  (Blue). The original red header's fill, <Token data={getTokenData('az.component.arizona-header.red.container.color')} />,
  uses <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.surface.primary-1')} /> (Red) instead,
  and stays until Arizona Bootstrap deprecates the red header.
- **Size:** the header is at least 50px tall, and exactly 50px below 992px.
- **Keyboard focus:** the logo and header buttons draw a 2px ring inside their edges,
  with 2px corners, in the surface's focus-ring color:
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-primary-2.focus-ring')} />, or
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-primary-1.focus-ring')} /> on the red header.
  Both are White.
- **Header buttons** (`.btn-arizona-header`, for adding search and menu toggles on
  mobile): text and icons in the surface's on color,
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-primary-2.default')} />, or
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-primary-1.default')} /> on the red header.
  Both are White. The buttons are at least 60px wide and as tall as the header.
- **Logo:** the wordmark is a White image file, so no token colors it.
- **Fixed on mobile:** when the header is pinned to the top of the screen below 992px,
  linked sections stop 58px below the top when scrolled to, so the header doesn't
  cover them.
