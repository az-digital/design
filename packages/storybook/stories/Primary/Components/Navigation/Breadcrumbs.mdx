# Breadcrumbs

A row of links from the home page to the current page, separated by `/`. The first item
can be Home, with a house icon before it.

## Colors

The breadcrumb's contract wires its parts straight to brand colors:

- **Behind it:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.white')} />.
- **Links, the current page and the house icon:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.azurite')} />.
  Linked items don't change color on hover; a 1px underline in the same color, 5px below
  the text's baseline, shows the hover instead.
- **Dividers:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.brand.bougainvillea')} />.

## Type and spacing

- **Type:** 16px Medium for every item, linked or current, with a 16px line height.
- **Padding:** 12px above and below, and 0.609rem (about 9.7px) to the left and right.
- **Dividers:** 8px of space on each side of the `/`.
- **Below:** 16px of space under the breadcrumb, and none from the lg breakpoint (992px) up.

## Home

- **Icon:** a 19.2px (1.2rem) outlined house, 2.4px before "Home", raised 2.4px so it
  lines up with the text the way it does on arizona.edu.
- **Hover:** the icon is part of the link, so the underline starts at the house's left
  edge and runs under "Home".

## Anatomy

The breadcrumb's contract (`breadcrumb.contract.json`) names its parts, and the token that sets
each part's design properties, at rest, on hover and from the lg breakpoint up. Colors point at
brand colors; sizes point at the breadcrumb's own tokens.
