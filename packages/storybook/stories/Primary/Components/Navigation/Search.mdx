# Search

The search form in a site's navigation: a text field with an icon button, White on the
Blue navbar it's designed for. It's built like an
<a href="./?path=/docs/primary-components-inputs-input-group--docs" target="_top">input group</a>,
but its look is its own, so it has its own contract (`search.contract.json`).

<Canvas of={SearchStories.Default} sourceState="none" />

The search form above is the current release. It matches this page.

## Design decisions

- **Colors:** text, placeholder, border, divider and icon are
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.white')} />, on the Blue it's designed for. The
  icon turns <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.rain')} /> on hover, and the border and
  divider turn Rain while the field or button has focus, fading over 150ms, ease-in-out.
- **Height and corners:** 35px tall with <Token data={getTokenData('az.radius.1')} /> (4px)
  corners below 992px, and 43px with <Token data={getTokenData('az.radius.3')} /> (8px)
  from 992px up.
- **Border:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.border.width.md')} /> (2px), around the field
  and button together, with a divider of the same width between them.
- **Field:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.spacing.075')} /> (6px) of padding above and
  below its text and <Token data={getTokenData('az.spacing.150')} /> (12px) to each side;
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.font-size.root')} /> (16px)
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.font-weight.normal')} /> (Regular) type on a 22px line.
- **Button:** an icon only, 24px, with 9px of padding around it.
- **Form:** up to 265px wide, with 5px above and below it and 15px to each side.
- **Small screens:** the button that shows the search form has a keyboard focus ring in
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.fg.rain')} />,
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.focus-ring.width.sm')} /> (1px) wide.
