# Foundations overview

This page establishes the shared foundation for how the Arizona Digital system presents and explains design choices to the university community.

## Goals

- explain the role of foundational principles in everyday design work
- connect accessibility and content guidance to design decisions
- give pages a consistent structure through shared breakpoints, grids, and spacing
- define a clear starting point for future design system expansion

The Foundations section frames the principles behind the design system and the guidance that structures all product work.
