# Design system terminology

This dictionary explains terms used in the Arizona Digital design system.

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th scope="col">Term</th>
      <th scope="col">Definition</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <th scope="row">Component</th>
      <td>A reusable part of a website, such as a button, card, or form field, that provides a specific function or content pattern.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <th scope="row">Foundation</th>
      <td>Shared design guidance and resources, such as accessibility, layout, and content guidance, that support the rest of the design system.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <th scope="row">Primitive</th>
      <td>A basic design value, such as a color or spacing amount, used to build more specific design choices.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <th scope="row">Token</th>
      <td>A named, reusable design value, such as a color, spacing amount, or font size, that helps keep designs consistent.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
