# Typography

Text styles for running copy and headings (`az.typography.*`), and the list styles that go with
them (`az.component.list.*`). Text colors aren't part of these styles: they come from the surface
the text sits on.

## Typefaces

- **Sans serif:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.font-family.sans')} />, Proxima Nova. Body copy and
  headings use it by default.
- **Serif:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.font-family.serif')} />, Garamond Premier Pro, for pages
  that opt into serif headings.

## Copy

- **Body:** 18px on a 28px line, Regular.
- **Lead:** 20px on a 30px line, Medium, for an introductory paragraph.
- **Small:** 16px on a 24px line, Regular.

<Specimen>
  <p className="lead">Lead copy introduces a page or section, a step larger than body copy.</p>
  <p>Body copy is the text most people read most of the time. It's 18px, on a 28px line, so long passages stay comfortable to read on any screen.</p>
  <p><small>Small copy is for captions, notes and fine print.</small></p>
</Specimen>

## Headings

Every heading is Semibold, with no letter spacing. h1–h3 get larger from the lg breakpoint (992px)
up; h4–h6 stay the same at every screen size.

- **h1:** 48px on a 56px line; 64px on a 74px line from 992px up.
- **h2:** 38px on a 46px line; 52px on a 62px line from 992px up.
- **h3:** 30px on a 38px line; 40px on a 50px line from 992px up.
- **h4:** 28px on a 36px line.
- **h5:** 22px on a 30px line.
- **h6:** 18px on a 26px line.

Space around a heading scales with its size: 1.042 times its size above it, and 0.667 times below
(<Token data={getTokenData('az.typography.heading.margin-top')} />,
<Token data={getTokenData('az.typography.heading.margin-bottom')} />). h1 has 0.667 times its size
above it too, so its spacing is even.

<Specimen>
  <h1>Heading 1</h1>
  <h2>Heading 2</h2>
  <h3>Heading 3</h3>
  <h4>Heading 4</h4>
  <h5>Heading 5</h5>
  <h6>Heading 6</h6>
</Specimen>

### Serif headings

Headings are sans serif unless a page opts into a serif style, which uses
<Token data={getTokenData('az.typography.heading.serif.font.family')} />:

- **Serif:** every heading level is serif.
- **Mixed:** h1–h3 are serif; h4–h6 stay sans serif.

<Specimen>
  <div className="az-headings-serif">
    <h2>Serif: Heading 2</h2>
    <h5>Serif: Heading 5</h5>
  </div>
  <div className="az-headings-mixed">
    <h2>Mixed: Heading 2</h2>
    <h5>Mixed: Heading 5</h5>
  </div>
</Specimen>

## Lists

Two bulleted list styles for body copy, each with a 20px marker centered on the item's first line,
16px before the text. Items are 24px apart. A nested list sits 14px below its item, with its own
items 14px apart.

- **Focus points:** a <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-default.accent')} />
  (Bougainvillea) disc with a four-pointed star cut into it, and a small
  <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-default.highlight')} /> (Rain) star at its center.
  It's the same on every surface.
- **Checkmarks:** a disc with a check, <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-default.icon')} />
  (Arroyo Blue) on the default surface, and <Token data={getTokenData('az.color.semantic.on-brand.accent')} />
  (Rain) on the brand surface, where Arroyo Blue is too dark to read.

The markers ship with the tokens package as masks, in `assets/markers/`: `focus-point.svg` and
`focus-point-highlight.svg` (drawn on top), and `checkmark.svg`. The color tokens paint them.
