# Typography

Brand typefaces apply to body text, headings, and components. Shared decisions, such as the
typefaces and body copy, are tokens. The rest is in contracts for body copy, headings, sidebar headings, and lists.
Text colors aren't part of these styles. They come from the background the text is on.

The examples on this page use the current release, so until its updates land they don't match
every value here.

## Typefaces

- **Sans serif:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.font-family.sans-serif')} />, Proxima Nova. Body
  copy and headings use it by default.
- **Serif:** <Token data={getTokenData('az.font-family.serif')} />, Garamond Premier Pro, for
  pages that use serif headings.

## Copy

- **Body:** 18px on a 28px line, Regular.
- **Lead:** 20px on a 30px line, Medium, for an introductory paragraph.
- **Small:** 16px on a 24px line, Regular, for captions and fine print.

<Example>
  <p className="lead">Lead copy introduces a page or section, a step larger than body copy.</p>
  <p>Body copy is the text most people read most of the time. It's 18px on a 28px line, so long passages stay easy to read.</p>
  <p><small>Small copy is for captions, notes and fine print.</small></p>
</Example>

## Headings

Every heading is Semibold, with no letter spacing. h1–h3 get larger from the lg breakpoint
(992px) up. h4–h6 stay the same at every screen size.

Space around a heading scales with its size: 1.042 times its size above it, and 0.667 times
below. h1 has 0.667 times its size above it too, so its spacing is even.

### Serif headings

Headings are sans serif unless a page opts into a serif style:

- **Serif:** every heading level is serif.
- **Mixed:** h1–h3 are serif. h4–h6 stay sans serif.

### Sidebar headings

Headings in a sidebar are smaller, and always sans serif, even on pages with serif or mixed
headings. They're Medium, on a line 1.2 times their size. Their spacing is the same as other
headings.

## Lists

Two bulleted list styles for body copy. Each has a 20px marker centered on the item's first
line, 16px before the text. Items are 24px apart. A nested list sits 14px below its item, with
its own items 14px apart.

- **Focus points:** a Bougainvillea disc with a four-pointed star cut into it, and a small Rain
  star at its center. It's the same on every background.
- **Checkmarks:** a disc with a check. It's Arroyo Blue on light backgrounds, and Rain on dark
  backgrounds, where Arroyo Blue is too dark to read.

The marker shapes are images that each implementation ships. The contract sets their size and
colors.
