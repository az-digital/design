## Initial hierarchy

- Foundations introduces the design rules and writing guidance that support the rest of the system.
- Components organizes the reusable building blocks teams can apply across experiences.
- The system remains oriented toward web-first usage and avoids duplicating print or marketing guidance that belongs outside the design system.
