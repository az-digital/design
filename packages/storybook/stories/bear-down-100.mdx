# Bear Down 100

This section captures the university-specific guidance for campaign and toolkit assets that are outside the core design system yet still part of the broader design ecosystem.

## Included guidance

- Overview
- Timeline and lifecycle
- Toolkits and campaigns

## Purpose

- Define the lifecycle and expiration of campaign assets.
- Distinguish toolkits from campaigns and from the core design system.
- Provide a clear reference point for university teams working on branded marketing and campaign assets.
