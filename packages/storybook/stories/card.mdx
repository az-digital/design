# Card

Card is a flexible content container — a bordered box with optional header, image,
title/subtitle/text/links, and footer. Purely presentational: no real interactive
behavior, so unlike Accordion/Tabs, this component is hand-rolled rather than wrapping
`react-bootstrap`.

## Content types

- **Titles, text, and links** — `title`/`subtitle`/`text`/`links`.
- **Image cap** (`image`) — `.card-img-top`, matching Arizona Bootstrap's own naming
  (top only; there's no `.card-img-bottom` prop here — not demonstrated as a distinct
  need yet).
- **Header and footer** — `header`/`footer`.

## Not covered by this component (yet)

Arizona Bootstrap's cards docs page covers considerably more ground than this first
pass. Deliberately deferred, not silently dropped:

- **List groups inside a card** (`.list-group.list-group-flush`) and the **"Kitchen
  sink"** example combining image + body + list group + links.
- **Navigation inside a card header** (`.nav-tabs`/`.nav-pills` in `.card-header`) —
  ties into [`Nav`](?path=/docs/components-nav--docs)/[`Tabs`](?path=/docs/components-tabs--docs);
  `custom/_card.scss` has real styling fixes specifically for this combination.
- **Image overlays**, **horizontal cards**.
- **Layout patterns**: card groups, CSS-grid cards, masonry.
- **Sizing** via grid markup, utilities, or custom CSS; **text alignment**;
  **background and color** variants.
- **Borderless and Clickable Cards**, and **Title on Image** — both confirmed
  **Arizona-custom** in the docs ("Custom Arizona Bootstrap Styling"), plus the
  deprecated `.card-clickable`/`.card-landing-grid` classes in
  `scss/deprecated/_card.scss`.

## Anatomy

The card's contract (`card.contract.json`) records its border, the padding of its header, body and footer, and the space below its title. Every value comes from Arizona Bootstrap's compiled CSS. Its colors aren't in the contract yet: where Arizona Bootstrap uses its gray neutrals rather than brand colors, those are a design decision still to make. The header and footer fill is one of them.
